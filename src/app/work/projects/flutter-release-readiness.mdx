## The product goal

A creative networking mobile app needed to help people build profiles, showcase work, discover talent, and communicate. The product also needed a reliable route from implementation to an approved iOS release.

## My contribution

My Flutter development and release work covered Firebase-backed authentication and profiles, real-time chat, push notifications, subscriptions, testing, and App Store submission.

I worked through production configuration and review requirements as part of delivering the application, rather than treating submission as a separate final task.

## Resolving a launch blocker

The App Store review required stronger controls for user-generated content. I added reporting and user-blocking functionality, improved the moderation and removal flow, reviewed the requirements, and resubmitted the app.

## Delivered outcome

The app was approved and went live on the App Store. Addressing the review requirements enabled the product to reach real users and gave the client a working release to continue improving.

## Engineering and business considerations

The work connected implementation quality with launch readiness: user safety controls, authentication, subscriptions, production configuration, and a usable core journey all had to work together. A functional codebase became an approved mobile product.

Client and product identities are kept private in this case study. Source code is not publicly distributed.
